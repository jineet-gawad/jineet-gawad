# Dr. Jineetkumar Gawad

### Computational and Medicinal Chemistry | Drug Discovery

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of KwaZulu-Natal, Durban, South Africa. My research combines computational chemistry and small-molecule synthesis to support drug discovery, with a particular interest in tuberculosis and antibacterial agents.

## Research interests

- Structure-based drug design and molecular docking
- Molecular dynamics simulations and protein–ligand analysis
- Pharmacophore modelling, virtual screening and QSAR
- Density functional theory calculations
- Integration of computational predictions with experimental research

## Research tools

Schrödinger Maestro, Glide, Phase, Desmond, PyMOL, AutoDock Vina, Gaussian and ORCA.

## About this portfolio

I am building a collection of documented scripts and workflows for molecular visualisation and computational chemistry.

My first project in preparation focuses on ligand-centred protein surface visualisation and distance-based residue analysis using PyMOL.

## Opportunities and collaboration

I am interested in postdoctoral opportunities and research collaborations in computational chemistry and computer-aided drug discovery.
